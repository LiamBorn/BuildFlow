**The first BuildFlow for Mac.** Your MacBook's notch becomes a small BuildFlow window.

- **A greeting** when you open the lid or unlock: "Good morning", with one line about your day.
- **Your inbox**: notifications, upcoming jobs, meetings, and what's waiting on you. Press Control + Option (left side) to open BuildFlow in the notch, or hover it.
- **Voice**: hold Control + Option to talk, and ask about your schedule. Nothing changes until you press Accept.
- **Updates** arrive on their own from now on. You can also choose Check for Updates… in the menu bar.
