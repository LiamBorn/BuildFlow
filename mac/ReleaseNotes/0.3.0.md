**A new look for the notch.**

- **Black and glowing.** The dropdown is black again, with icon tabs beside the camera, your list and Today side by side, and a soft light that runs round the edge as it opens.
- **A greeting first, every time.** Press Control + Option (left side): it says good morning, good afternoon or good evening, then opens your day.
- **Alerts and countdowns** glow in their own colour, and meetings and jobs count down with a ring.
- **Dark is the new default.** Light is still in the gear menu.
- **Check for Updates…** is in the gear menu too.
